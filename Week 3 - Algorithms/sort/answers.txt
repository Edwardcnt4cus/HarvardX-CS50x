sort1 uses: Bubble Sort

How do you know?: It’s fastest on sorted input and slowest on random, which is typical for Bubble Sort.

How do you know?: It takes about the same time on all inputs, which is how Selection Sort behaves—it always scans the whole list no matter the order.
